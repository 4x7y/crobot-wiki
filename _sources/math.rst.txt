Math, curves and surfaces
=========================

Link target: ``crobot``. Namespace: ``crobot::math``. Include the narrow header
you use, or the generated ``crobot/math/math.hpp`` umbrella. See :doc:`api/math`
for every header and its implementation.

Linear algebra and rotations
----------------------------

``matrix.hpp`` defines Eigen-based vector/matrix aliases, spatial vectors,
Jacobians, sparse Jacobians, transforms and point-cloud arrays. ``rotation.hpp``
and ``quaternion.hpp`` supply rotation operations and the quaternion alias.
CRobot installs Eigen plugins from ``math/detail/``; include CRobot headers
before Eigen if your code needs the extra conversion methods.

Read :doc:`conventions` for quaternion order, transform direction and angular-
first spatial vectors. ``calculate_jacb_inv`` provides damped and SVD-based
Jacobian inversion; choose damping with respect to the scale and conditioning
of the problem, rather than treating an inverse as exact at a singularity.

Geometry families
-----------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 32 68

   * - Family
     - Use
   * - ``Curve``, ``CurveSegment``
     - Parametric curves and restricted parameter intervals.
   * - ``BSplineCurve``, ``RationalCurve``
     - Spline and weighted rational curves, with evaluation and derivatives.
   * - ``Surface``, ``SurfacePatch``
     - Parametric surfaces and bounded patches used by contact constraints.
   * - ``BSplineSurface``, ``RationalSurface``, ``SplineSurface``
     - Surface representations for fitting/evaluation and contact geometry.
   * - ``Plane``, ``Sphere``, ``Ellipsoid``
     - Analytic geometry usable by the surface/contact machinery.
   * - ``SurfacePatchSwitch``
     - Switching among surface patches in contact-related workflows.
   * - ``polynomial.hpp``, ``bspline.hpp``, ``spline.hpp``
     - Lower-level polynomial and spline calculations and helpers.

The analytical ``math::Geometry`` family is distinct from triangle-mesh data
in :doc:`geometry` and HPP-FCL shapes in :doc:`collision`. Pick the representation
required by the consuming API. Surface coordinates are parameters, not
necessarily distances; use derivatives and normals from the same representation
as the contact constraint.

For examples with explicit control points, knot vectors, derivatives and
expected values, read ``unittests/unit/test_math_bspline_curve.cpp``,
``test_math_bspline_surface.cpp``, ``test_math_rational_curve.cpp`` and
``test_math_rational_surface.cpp``. ``tool_bspline_visualization`` provides an
interactive example. Do not start from ``parametric_surface.hpp`` or
``polynomial_surface.hpp``: their proposed classes are commented out.

Filters, graphs and sampling
----------------------------

``Differentiator`` and ``LowPass`` support signal processing; use a consistent
sample period with the producer of the data. ``Random`` wraps sampling.
``Graph`` wraps graph storage, while ``Metric``, ``NearestNeighbors``,
``LinearNearestNeighbors`` and ``KdtreeNearestNeighbors`` support search.
``StateSpace<T>`` defines sampling, interpolation, distance and validity for
planners; its validity methods are part of the application contract.
``LCPSolver`` contains complementarity-solving utilities.

Use ``test_math_graph``, ``test_math_kdtree``, ``test_math_surface`` and
``test_math_polynomial`` as focused checks when changing these utilities. The
``detail/`` headers implement templates and Eigen plugins; they are included by
the public headers and are not independent application entry points.
