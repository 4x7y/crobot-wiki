Mesh geometry and preparation
=============================

Link target: ``crobot-geometry``. Namespace: ``crobot::geometry``. This module
uses Open3D triangle meshes and has no dependency on patch-tree generation or
grasp-search policy. Include ``crobot/geometry/geometry.hpp`` or a specific
header from :doc:`api/geometry`.

Load and prepare a surface
--------------------------

.. code-block:: cpp

   auto mesh = crobot::geometry::MeshIO::load_mesh("object.stl");
   crobot::geometry::MeshPreparationOptions options;
   auto prepared = crobot::geometry::prepare_mesh(*mesh, options);
   const auto& report = prepared.report;

This fragment needs ``geometry/mesh_io.hpp`` and
``geometry/mesh_preprocessor.hpp``. ``MeshIO`` also saves and visualizes meshes;
load/save failures throw exceptions. Apply coordinate scaling before constructing
contact geometry or choosing length-dependent tolerances.

Preparation can weld exact duplicate vertices, remove duplicate/degenerate
faces, orient triangles and nested shells, and smooth normals while respecting
features. ``MeshPreparationOptions`` controls these choices.
``MeshPreparationReport`` records counts, boundary/nonmanifold diagnostics,
orientation conflicts, ambiguous shells, normal fallbacks and refinement work.
Inspect these fields before assuming an imported surface is suitable for
contact analysis. Preparation is not a guarantee that every input becomes a
closed, manifold object.

Keep the correspondence
-----------------------

``PreparedMesh`` carries the mesh and original/prepared correspondences:
``original_vertex_to_prepared``, ``face_source_ids``,
``original_face_to_prepared``, ``original_face_to_prepared_faces`` and
``original_face_disposition``. Refinement can map one original face to several
prepared faces. Use the one-to-many map when tracking labels or observations
through refinement; do not assume face numbers survived cleanup.

``MeshUtils`` supplies geometry helpers and triangle extraction.
``GaussianCurvature`` estimates curvature, while ``mesh_refinement`` supports
subdivision with provenance. See ``test_mesh_preprocessor.cpp`` and
``test_geometry_module.cpp`` for degenerate surfaces and mapping checks.

Saved patch geometry
--------------------

``PatchMeshData`` loads the shared surface/label representation used by older
saved-data experiments. Its folder contains ``patch_offsets.csv`` and numbered
partition folders with ``vertices.csv``, ``faces.csv`` and ``face_labels.csv``.
Vertex attributes are loaded separately. ``MeshPatches`` adds vertex neighbors,
boundary distances and representative patch centers; it requires the normals
and vertex labels as well. ``MeshVertex`` stores individual contact candidates.

Share ``MeshPatches::get_mesh_data()`` with
``PatchTree::build_level_graphs`` when combining saved geometry and hierarchy.
This keeps adjacency tied to actual surface connectivity. These CSV-based
loaders are not loaders for the new ``patch_tree`` example's ``result.yaml``;
the YAML writer currently has no matching public round-trip loader.

Continue with :doc:`patch_tree` to generate a hierarchy from a fresh mesh, or
:doc:`grasping` to evaluate contacts in saved patch geometry.
