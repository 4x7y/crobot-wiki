Hybrid Control¶

Robot Dynamics¶

Dynamics in the configuration space:

\[M \ddot{\theta} + C(\theta)\dot{\theta} + G(\theta) + J^\top w = \tau_{\rm free}\]

Compact configuration representation \(q \in \mathbb{R}^m\)

Apply the transmission matrix:

\[\begin{split}\tau &= Q\tau_{\rm free} \\ \dot{q} &= Q\dot{\theta}\end{split}\]

and now we have the robot dynamics in terms of the compact configuration space variables

\[QM Q^\top \ddot{q} + QC(\theta)\dot{\theta} + QG(\theta) + QJ^\top w = \tau\]

The task space velocity and configuration velocity has the following relationship

\[\begin{split}{}^c v &= \tilde{R}^\top J Q^\top \dot{q} \\ {}^c \dot{x} &= \tilde{T} {}^c v = \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J Q^\top \dot{q}\end{split}\]

where

\[\begin{split}{}^c x = \begin{bmatrix} {}^c p \\ {}^c \psi \end{bmatrix}, \quad \tilde{R} = \begin{bmatrix} R & 0 \\ 0 & R \end{bmatrix} , \quad \tilde{T} = \begin{bmatrix} I_{3, 3} & 0 \\ 0 & T(\psi) \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

and \({}^c \dot{\psi} = T({}^c \psi) {}^c \omega\).

Let \(A = \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J Q^\top\), we have

\[\begin{split}{}^c\dot{x} &= A \dot{q} \\ {}^c\ddot{x} &= \dot{A} \dot{q} + A \ddot{q}\end{split}\]

Thus

\[\ddot{q} = (SA)^{-1} \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}' - S \dot{A} \dot{q} \Big)\]

and the robot dynamics in terms of \({}^c\ddot{x}'\) is represented as

\[\tau = QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}' - S \dot{A} Q \dot{\theta} \Big) + QC(\theta)\dot{\theta} + QG(\theta) + QJ^\top \tilde{R}\, {}^cw\]

Controller Design¶

Because of the geometry constraint

\[S S_f = 0\]

The hybrid controller is writtern as

\[\begin{split}\tau_{\rm ctrl} &= QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} \Big( S S_v^\top {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - S \dot{A} Q \dot{\theta} \Big) + QC(\theta)\dot{\theta} + QG(\theta) \\ &\qquad + QJ^\top \tilde{R} \bar{S}_f^\top \bar{S}_f \, {}^c f + QJ^\top \tilde{R} S_f^\top \, {}^c f_{\rm res}'\end{split}\]

Error Dynamics¶

Equite hybrid controller with robot dynamics

\[\tau_{\rm ctrl} = \tau\]

we have

\[QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) + QJ^\top \tilde{R} S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

Left multiplying \(S_f S^\top SA(QMQ^\top)^{-1}\) to the above equation, we have

\[S_f S^\top S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x} \Big) + S_f S^\top SA(QMQ^\top)^{-1} QJ^\top \tilde{R} S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

By showing that

\[\begin{split}S_f S^\top S S_v^\top &= S_f \begin{bmatrix} S_v^\top & S_f^\top \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} S_v \\ S_f \end{bmatrix} S_v^\top \\ &= S_f \begin{bmatrix} S_v^\top & S_f^\top \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} I_{n_v, n_v} \\ 0_{n_f, n_v} \end{bmatrix} \\ &= S_f S_v^\top \\ &= 0\end{split}\]

we can get rid of the first term of the error dyanmics, Thus

\[S_f S^\top SA(QMQ^\top)^{-1} QJ^\top \tilde{R} S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

Substitute the expression of \(A = \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J Q^\top\) into the equation above, we have

\[S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J Q^\top (QMQ^\top)^{-1} QJ^\top \tilde{R} S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

Using the fact that \(\tilde{T}S_f^\top = S_f^\top\) and \(S^\top S S_f^\top\), we have

\[S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J^\top Q^\top (QMQ^\top)^{-1} Q J \tilde{R} \tilde{T}^\top S^\top S S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

Observing that \(Q J \tilde{R} \tilde{T}^\top S^\top S S_f^\top\) is the transpose matrix of \(S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J^\top Q^\top\), we rewrite the above equation in the following form

\[\Big( S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J^\top Q^\top \Big) (QMQ^\top)^{-1} \Big(S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J^\top Q^\top \Big)^\top \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0.\]

If the Jacobian \(J\) is not evaluated at a singular configuration, the term \(S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J^\top Q^\top\) has full row rank and

\[\Big( S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J^\top Q^\top \Big) (QMQ^\top)^{-1} \Big(S_f S^\top S \tilde{T} \tilde{R}^\top J^\top Q^\top \Big)^\top\]

is positive defninite. Therefore, we prove that

\[{}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' = 0.\]

Now we get back to the original error dyanmics equation:

\[QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) + QJ^\top \tilde{R} S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

By left multiplying \(S_v S^\top (SA)^{-\top}\) to both sides of the equation, we have

\[(SA)^{-\top} QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) + (SA)^{-\top} QJ^\top \tilde{R} S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

Now we apply the fact that

\[S_f^\top = \tilde{T}^\top S^\top S S_f^\top\]

we have

\[(SA)^{-\top} QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) + (SA)^{-\top} QJ^\top \tilde{R} \tilde{T}^\top S^\top S S_f^\top \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) = 0\]

and by substituting the expression \(QJ^\top \tilde{R} \tilde{T}^\top ` to :math:`A^\top\), we have

\[\begin{split}S_v S^\top (SA)^{-\top} QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) \qquad \qquad & \\ + S_v S^\top \Big( A^\top S^\top \Big)^{-1} \Big(A^\top S^\top \Big) S S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) &= 0 \\ \Rightarrow \quad S_v S^\top (SA)^{-\top} QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) \qquad \qquad & \\ + S_v S^\top S S_f^\top \, \Big( {}^c f_{\rm res}' - {}^c f' \Big) &= 0\end{split}\]

Since \(S_v S^\top S S_f^\top = 0\)

\[S_v S^\top (SA)^{-\top} QMQ^\top (SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) = 0\]

which is equivalent to

\[\Big((SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big)^\top \Big( QMQ^\top \Big) \Big((SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big) \Big( {}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' \Big) = 0\]

Again, if the Jacobian \(J\) is non-singular, the matrix

\[\Big((SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big)^\top \Big( QMQ^\top \Big) \Big((SA)^{-1} S S_v^\top \Big)\]

is positive definite, and therefore

\[{}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' - {}^c\ddot{x}' = 0\]

To achieve expoenetial error decay, we let

\[{}^c\ddot{x}_{\rm res}' = {}^c\ddot{x}_d' + K_{vp} {}^cx_e' + K_{vd} {}^c\dot{x}_e',\]

where the pose error is defined as \({}^cx_e' = {}^cx_d' - {}^cx'\).
